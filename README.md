## 🧑‍💻 Who am I?

I'm Firas, an AI/software engineer with a curiosity that extends beyond writing code. I'm interested in understanding how technology works, why engineering decisions matter, and how thoughtful software can solve real problems.

I enjoy taking an idea from a blank screen to a working application: exploring the problem, making technical decisions, designing the user experience, implementing the solution, and figuring out what it takes to make the result useful.

Over time, building personal projects has become more than a way to practice programming. It's how I challenge my assumptions, experiment with new technologies, discover my strengths, and become a better engineer.

I don't expect to know everything before I start building. I'd rather explore, experiment, encounter the hard parts, and learn enough to make better decisions the next time around.

**My goal is to become the kind of engineer who can turn complex ideas into useful, reliable, and thoughtfully designed products.**

## 🚀 What I'm working toward

- **Building real products:** Strengthening my software engineering skills by creating applications from the ground up.
- **Exploring AI engineering:** Learning about large language models, embeddings, retrieval-augmented generation (RAG), vector databases, AI agents, and the practical challenges of developing AI-powered applications.
- **Thinking like a product engineer:** Considering architecture, usability, maintainability, and real-world requirements-not just whether the code works.
- **Building my first AI micro-SaaS:** Bringing software engineering and AI together to explore how a useful idea can become a production-oriented product.
- **Continuing my academic journey:** Deepening my technical foundations and preparing for opportunities to grow through advanced study, research, and collaboration.

I'm drawn to work that requires curiosity, independent thinking, experimentation, and a willingness to learn beyond what's already familiar.
